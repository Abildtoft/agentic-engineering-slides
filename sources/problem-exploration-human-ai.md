# Problem exploration: humans and AI

Source: Atmo (@atmoio), X post, https://x.com/atmoio/status/2061916783309692989

The useful mental model: represent facts as nodes and correlations as edges. AI is strong at mapping correlations inside the known graph. Humans and science push the graph outward by finding new nodes.

For the talk, frame the optimistic version carefully: AI alone gives dense local exploration, not self-directed breadth. Humans alone explore broadly but sparsely. Humans plus AI can put more human-selected frontier points into play, then let AI deepen each one.

Useful line: humans find new nodes; AI maps the edges.
