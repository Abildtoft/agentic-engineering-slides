# Duolingo on the Changing PM Role

Aakash Gupta shared a Duolingo Chief Product Officer note on March 18, 2026.

Key lines:
- "A PM with a good idea can now get to a first draft faster than ever."
- PMs can visualize concepts, test directions, and move quickly.
- The role trends toward something more independent, more creative, and more exciting.
- The distance between having an idea and being able to show someone what it looks like has shrunk.

This is a clean product-side example of the talk's larger thesis: as implementation gets cheaper, product leverage moves toward intent, speed of validation, and judgment.
