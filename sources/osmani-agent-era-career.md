# The Agent-Era Career

Addy Osmani, published 6 July 2026.

Osmani frames career durability around work without an answer key: finding the right problem, deciding what is worth shipping, judging the output, and finishing beyond the machine-generated median. Agentic tools make implementation abundant, increasing the relative value of reputation, relationships, systems thinking, specification, verification, and ownership.

He warns that agents automate the repetitions that previously developed taste. Deliberate practice becomes necessary: work manually on selected hard problems, read code deeply, keep a wrong-answer log, and maintain an end-to-end mental model of at least one system. Autonomy is per task, not a status level; raise it for reversible, cheap-to-check work and lower it for costly errors.

The closing career model is director rather than fastest typist: scope the work, define done, calibrate trust, verify independently, and remain accountable for what ships.
