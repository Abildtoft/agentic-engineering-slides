# Linear Next

Karri Saarinen's March 24, 2026 launch thesis for Linear: issue tracking was built for a handoff model, but agentic teams need a shared system organized around context, intent, and execution. Useful as a category signal from a leading issue-tracking product, not neutral market research.

- Company-reported product metrics across Linear workspaces: coding agents are installed in 75%+ of Linear enterprise workspaces; agent-completed work grew 5x over the prior 3 months; agents authored nearly 25% of new issues.
- Best use in the talk: support claims about handoff compression, context becoming infrastructure, and the shift from backlog management toward context-to-execution systems.
