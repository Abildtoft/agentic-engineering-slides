# Why Product Sense Is the Only Product Skill That Will Matter in the AI Age

Within 1–7 years, competitive companies will use AI for all product activities — discovery, design, coding, testing, analytics. The specific AI tools will be equivalent across companies. Tools have historically never been a source of lasting competitive advantage. Therefore the differentiator can only be how you improve upon what those tools provide: human product judgment.

Doshi decomposes Product Sense into five integrated skills:

1. **Strong empathy** — understanding needs beyond what AI analysis surfaces
2. **Excellent simulation skills** — identifying future possibilities from domain expertise
3. **Stellar strategic thinking** — identifying resonant segments and differentiators
4. **Great taste** — selecting the right recommendations aligned with business goals
5. **Creative execution** — conceiving unique solutions competitors cannot replicate

This framework was articulated in October 2022, before ChatGPT's launch — not a reactive take on the AI wave, but a structural prediction about where human value concentrates when execution cost collapses.
