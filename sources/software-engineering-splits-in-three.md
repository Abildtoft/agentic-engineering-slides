# Software Engineering Splits in Three

Matteo Collina argues that AI-assisted coding shifts the bottleneck from implementation to judgment, splitting what was a unified profession into three distinct market tiers:

**Tier 1 — Technology Companies:** Software is the product. AI is a force multiplier for senior engineers. Emphasis on platform engineering, SRE, and upstream collaboration. Senior engineers review AI-generated code for production readiness rather than writing it.

**Tier 2 — Large Enterprises:** Banks, insurers, retailers — dependent on software but not software-first. Rely on platforms with built-in guardrails. Adopt "fractional senior expertise" consulting models. The consulting shift: from body shopping to judgment.

**Tier 3 — Mid-Market & Small Business:** Newly accessible custom software market. The "software plumber" — local developers solving specific business problems. Less distributed systems, more business understanding.

Key implications:
- Platform engineering becomes essential: "guardrails over gates" as the safety mechanism for faster code delivery
- SRE role elevates as the last line of defense
- Talent pipeline crisis: junior hiring must continue but requires rethinking — mentorship through review, not implementation tickets
- Consulting model transforms from implementation to judgment

> "The cost of software production is trending towards zero" — Malte Ubl, CTO at Vercel
