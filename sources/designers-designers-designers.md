# Designers! Designers! Designers!

Carly Ayres on the designer market paradox: companies desperately seek talent while designers fear AI-driven obsolescence. The real shortage isn't candidates — it's strategic designers capable of shipping outcomes across product, engineering, and business domains.

AI handles visual production and design system assembly. What remains: understanding users, framing the right problems, and translating complexity into experiences people trust.

Companies now demand "Super ICs" — senior individual contributors who set direction, make autonomous decisions, ship to production, and measure results. This consolidates roles previously held by separate designers, product managers, and engineers.

Key quotes:

> "Once everyone can make an app, we will remember that the hard part about apps isn't making the app." — Preston Attebery

> "Design used to be cheap, at the end of a service line. Now, MVPs are made by designers." — Sebastian Speier

> "People are finally starting to realize how few truly great designers there are, and how disproportionately valuable they are." — Benji Taylor

The future belongs to designers who prove they can navigate blurred boundaries across disciplines — and to companies that distinguish execution-level candidates from strategic hybrid practitioners.
