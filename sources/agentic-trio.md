# The Agentic Trio

Daniel Westheide and Fabian Walther, INNOQ. The concept appeared in Westheide's article "The Agentic Trio" on the INNOQ blog (May 2026); the agentictrio.org microsite with the theses launched on 22 October 2026.

## The argument

- **The trio takes on delivery.** "The Agentic Trio extends the Product Trio to include delivery: PM, UX and engineering share responsibility for both." One of the theses is titled "The Agentic Trio has no delivery-only roles."
- **Freed capacity is a choice.** "The obvious move would be to put the freed capacity into more output." The theses argue against it ("The freed capacity is a strategic choice"; "More output destabilises the system"): "Teams that check whether a problem is real before they build anything build less that isn't needed."
- **The goal:** "The goal is better outcomes for users and organisations, not more generated code."

## Deck fit

- **Section 5, "The fastest way to build is to find the cheapest way to learn" (presenter notes).** The same argument from a consultancy's practice: capacity agents free up belongs in validating problems, not in producing more code.
- **Section 2, RolesBlur (presenter notes).** "No delivery-only roles" is the convergence claim from the trio's side, without merging the disciplines. Contrast with Thorsten Ball's maximalist "the triad of PM/Design/Eng will disappear" ([[thorsten-ball-future-of-software-development]]).
