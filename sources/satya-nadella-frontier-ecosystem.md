# Satya Nadella - A Frontier Without An Ecosystem Is Not Stable

Satya Nadella frames the AI transition as the first platform shift where firms can create a real cognitive loop between people and digital systems. The strategic issue is not choosing the best model. It is whether an organization can turn workflows, domain knowledge, and accumulated judgment into AI systems that improve with use while preserving control over its IP.

Core concepts:

- **Human capital**: knowledge, judgment, relationships, ingenuity, and pattern recognition.
- **Token capital**: the AI capability a firm builds and owns.
- Human capital becomes more valuable as token capital grows because humans set goals, connect domains, and recognize which patterns matter.
- The durable asset is the learning loop on top of models: workflows, traces, private evals, reinforcement environments, knowledge bases, and harnesses.
- The sovereignty test: a company should be able to swap out a generalist model without losing the company-specific expertise embedded in its system.
- The ecosystem argument: if all value accrues to a few frontier models, industries will see their knowledge commoditized and the political economy will not tolerate it.
