# Your parallel Agent limit

Addy Osmani, published 7 April 2026.

Running more agents increases generation throughput but not human cognitive bandwidth. Each thread carries its own problem framing, architecture, trust calibration, and risk of silent drift. The hidden cost is background vigilance plus repeated mental-model reloads.

Osmani argues that supervision scales further than understanding, and the gap becomes comprehension debt. The relevant ceiling varies with task complexity, brief quality, session length, and the operator's available attention; it is not a fixed number supplied by the tool.

His practical method is to time-box sessions, tighten scopes, start below the apparent maximum, and watch review confidence rather than agent count. In his own workflow, a few focused threads produce more mergeable output than a larger half-supervised fleet.
