# What Will Be Required

Victor Savkin argues that software engineering is changing unevenly but decisively: some organizations are already operating in the new mode, while others are still years behind. The engineers who will come out ahead are already practicing four things: autonomous decision-making, product ownership, working in small self-directed teams, and constant experimentation with new AI-native development workflows.

His point is not that this is easy. It is cognitively demanding to own outcomes, make the decisions seniors or managers used to absorb, and keep adapting to agentic tooling. But those demands are becoming the job, not an optional edge.
