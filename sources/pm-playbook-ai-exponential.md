# The PM Playbook on the AI Exponential

George from prodmgmt.world summarizes the operating-model shift for PMs with a simple contrast:

- Before AI, product, design, and engineering often behaved like a waterfall.
- With AI, the work moves into a tighter overlap zone where product, design, and engineering act on the same loop.

His added point is important: the role does not disappear, but the leverage shifts away from translation-heavy handoffs and toward process design, coordination, and decision ownership.
