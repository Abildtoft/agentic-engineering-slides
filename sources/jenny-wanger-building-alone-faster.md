# Building Alone, Faster

Jenny Wanger (author and product coach), jennywanger.com, 1 October 2026. Write-up of "Blurred Lines", a panel on what AI is doing to the product trio, hosted by AI & Product Colorado in Denver on 14 September 2026. Moderator Lauri Hofherr; panelists Jake Taylor (Senior Director of Product Design and Research, JumpCloud), Riley Scardina (Code Talent), Jason Fletchall and Wanger.

## The argument

- **"AI is still single-player."** The line is Wanger's, from a LinkedIn post the moderator read aloud to open the panel. The post closes: "The trio that used to build the thing together has stopped building it together."
- **Faster, and solitary.** AI makes each role faster, but each solves its own piece of the problem alone.
- **Deferred collaboration is debt.** Taylor: "a debt that you rack up." His concrete case: PMs who build prototypes outside the design system leave his team to backtrack and clean up. Wanger: "You are racking up debt. And it feels so easy to do it single player."
- **Prototypes are not products.** Taylor: "Prototypes are meant to be just that. They can't go into production." Wanger: "We don't ship prototypes. We ship hardened products."
- **Engineering still gates shipping.** Taylor: "Engineers are the gatekeepers." Wanger: engineering and QA set up the systems that let everyone ship code safely, which takes real test coverage and AI-assisted code review.
