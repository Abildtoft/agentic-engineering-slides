# Alfred Lin on Playing Offense in a Period of Compounding Change

In a March 27, 2026 X post, Alfred Lin shared a message from one of his portfolio company CEOs and said it reflected what founders and CEOs should be communicating right now.

The core framing is simple:
- The biggest risk is no longer only making the wrong decision.
- The bigger risk is moving too slowly while the world changes around you.
- Teams can respond defensively by protecting what exists, optimizing current systems, and waiting for clarity.
- Or they can respond offensively by learning faster than the environment changes, using new tools to solve old problems in better ways, and creating entirely new strategies and businesses.

This is useful for the talk because it reframes AI adoption as a leadership and operating-model question, not a tooling question. The strategic mistake is not just poor execution. It is caution that hardens into drift.
