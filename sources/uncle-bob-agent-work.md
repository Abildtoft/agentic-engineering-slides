# Robert C. Martin — the work of directing agents

Robert C. Martin describes agentic software development as both highly productive and demanding, focused work. Even while barely reading the generated code, he says the process draws on his full software-engineering and problem-solving skill set.

The quote is useful because it rejects the false choice between productivity and rigor: less direct code-writing does not mean less engineering. The work moves outward into directing agents, defining constraints, judging results, and solving the underlying problem.
