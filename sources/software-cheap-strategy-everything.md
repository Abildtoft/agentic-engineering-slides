# When Software Becomes Cheap, Strategy Becomes Everything

Martin Eriksson, The Decision Stack — March 2026

---

Core thesis: as AI drives the cost of building software toward zero, competitive advantage shifts from technological capability to strategic clarity. Companies applying AI only at the feature level will lose to those rethinking their entire business models.

---

## The Jevons Paradox

Both AI hype and panic focus on the wrong timeframe. When costs collapse, demand explodes rather than contracts.

> "We tend to overestimate the short-term impact of new technologies while underestimating their long-term effects."

**Radiology case study:** Geoffrey Hinton predicted AI would eliminate radiologists within five years. Instead, radiologist salaries increased 44% (average $525,000), Mayo Clinic doubled its staff, AI mammography screening detects 29% more cancers while reducing radiologist workload by 44%, and over 1,000 FDA-cleared AI radiology devices now exist.

> "AI didn't replace radiologists. It made imaging so much faster and cheaper that we started doing vastly more of it."

---

## Playing at the Wrong Stack Level

Most companies apply AI only at the "Opportunities" layer — adding features and chatbots. Winners rethink at the "Strategy" layer, fundamentally reconsidering their business models, pricing, and value propositions.

> "Speed without direction is just burning jet fuel on a runway... the bottleneck was never execution — it was always clarity."

> "What wasn't feasible before but now is? Product has always been good at managing feasibility risk. But that calculus has changed."

---

## Case Studies

**Intercom:** Built Fin (resolving 1M+ weekly tickets) and shifted to outcome-based pricing at $0.99 per resolution — reached $100M+ ARR in less than a year. Aligned incentives so the product had to actually work.

**Kive:** After ChatGPT launched, pivoted from asset management software to generative campaign creation tools, fundamentally reshaping their target market.

**Fujifilm:** Survived digital disruption not through superior technology but through clearer strategy.

---

## The Scale of Untapped Opportunity

- AI cost per token declining at ~10x annually
- 84% of Earth's population (8.1B people) have never used AI
- Only 0.3% have paid for AI
- 0.04% have tried AI coding tools

---

## Key Takeaways

Technology capability becomes table stakes. The real moat is knowing which problems are worth solving. Organizations should build for optionality — a "barbell strategy" that protects core operations while making small bets on radical alternatives.
