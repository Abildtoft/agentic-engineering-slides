# How Top PMs Increase Their Leverage with AI

Colin Matthews, guest post in Lenny's Newsletter, 30 June 2026.

## The product leverage ladder

"Product leverage closes the gap between what you want to build and what you can ship." Three rungs:

1. **Web-based prototypes** (Lovable, Replit, Magic Patterns). Good for communicating an idea, but the code is separate from the product's codebase, so little carries over.
2. **Code-based prototypes.** Claude Code or Codex working in the real codebase, so prototypes use the product's actual components and patterns instead of screenshots or long prompts.
3. **Agent-shipped pull requests.** An agent opens pull requests for small changes, and an engineer reviews and merges them.

The judgment that matters as much as technical skill is knowing whether a problem needs a doc, a prototype or a pull request. "As a PM, it does not make sense to spend time being a worse engineer than the rest of your team."

## Deck fit

- **Section 5, "The prototype is the new brief" (presenter notes).** The ladder is the slide's division of labour made concrete: the people closest to the domain climb to rung 2 or 3, and engineers review and harden what earns survival.
- **Section 2, RolesBlur (presenter notes).** "A worse engineer than the rest of your team" is the crisp form of "convergence of capabilities is not collapse of professions."
