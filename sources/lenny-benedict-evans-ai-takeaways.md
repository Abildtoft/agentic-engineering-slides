# Lenny Rachitsky / Benedict Evans AI Takeaways

Lenny Rachitsky summarized a Benedict Evans conversation on the current AI wave. Core framing: AI is comparable in importance to the internet or mobile, but still early enough that the winning companies, durable workflows, and highest-value use cases remain unclear.

Key ideas for the deck:

- New technology waves create new harm surfaces. The right response is safeguards and accountability, not panic or paralysis.
- Broad historical averages can be true while individual transitions still hurt. Automation can make society richer while specific workers, towns, and career ladders absorb real pain.
- The useful response to job anxiety is immersion: learn the tools deeply, understand what changes, and reposition toward the work that remains valuable.
- Accounting is a useful Jevons-style example: repeated automation did not eliminate the profession; it expanded the economically worthwhile surface area of accounting work.
- The key distinction is task vs. job. Some jobs collapse when their core task is automated, but many professions are paid for diagnosis, context, judgment, and accountability; the artifact is only the visible output.
