# PostHog on Product Management Skills Engineers Need

PostHog argues that, thanks to LLMs, figuring out what to build is becoming a bigger bottleneck than how to build it.

The piece frames three product-management skills engineers increasingly need:
- Providing context
- Running feedback loops and accountability loops
- Communicating toward action

This supports the talk's thesis that the lines between product and engineering are blurring. The leverage shifts toward judgment, end-to-end ownership, and turning context into decisions and shipped outcomes.
