This talk is a synthesis. Its argument, and the ideas behind several figures
and charts, build on other people's work. Addy Osmani is the strongest single
influence, and several visuals are my redraws and adaptations of ideas from
his work. Many other researchers and practitioners shaped individual sections
— the themes are on the slide, and I will name people as we go. The synthesis
and presentation are mine; the underlying frameworks remain theirs.
